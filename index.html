Chapter One

Founded With Purpose

C. Walker-Brooks & Associates Services Limited brings together decades of academic training and hands-on experience in construction, civil and structural engineering, and development project management.

The company's directors have contributed to local and international infrastructure projects—including bridges, roads, railways, airports, post-harvest facilities, and modern and historic monuments. The business was rebranded in 2022 to unite this experience in support of regional and international development.

"Experienced professionals working to complete every project efficiently, safely, and to the highest standards."

— Our Commitment

Chapter Three

What We Deliver

A comprehensive suite of professional services underpinned by deep sector expertise.

Project Management

Planning, organising, and executing projects from start to finish while managing budget, time, quality, and client expectations.

Architectural & 3D Drawings

Detailed architectural drawings and 3D visualisations that help clients understand, refine, and plan their spaces before construction begins.

Structural Engineering

Engineering reports, structural designs, drawings, and detailing developed for safety, stability, and applicable codes and regulations.

Building Quantities

Accurate building quantity surveys and cost estimates that support informed decisions and stronger budget control.

Construction & Civil Works

General construction and civil engineering works, including surveying, site planning, roads, and water-supply systems.

Electrical, Maintenance & Water Solutions

Electrical contracting, rainwater harvesting, HVAC, plumbing, electrical maintenance, repairs, and general property upkeep.

Supporting Capability: In-House Plan Printing

In-house large-format printing of architectural drawings, engineering plans and construction documents supports faster revisions, stronger document control and timely delivery of site-ready drawing sets.
